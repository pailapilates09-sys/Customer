Measurements and sources

First-load figures are rounded kilobytes (1 KB = 1,000 bytes). On 23 November 2026, each view opened in Chrome with a fresh set of asset URLs, in desktop and 390-pixel portrait viewports. We counted the page and its requested resources 3.2 seconds after load, before interaction. Network speed and device hardware also affect elapsed time.

The previous tour loaded 802 KB on desktop and 549 KB at phone width. The new tour uses smaller frames while you move and loads original detail when you pause. The chooser loads no animation frames. Switching the floor plan to 3D or moving through the tour requests more data.
